About Us

Built by drivers, for drivers

Hamdan Car Rental started with a single hatchback and a promise to make renting a car feel effortless. Today we serve five major cities with a growing, well-maintained fleet.

Our Story

Making every trip effortless

What began as a small family garage has grown into a rental service trusted by thousands of travellers. We believe renting a car shouldn't come with hidden fees, confusing paperwork, or unreliable vehicles — so we built our entire process around transparency and speed.

Every car in our fleet is inspected, cleaned and serviced between rentals, and our team is on call 24/7 for anything the road throws at you.
